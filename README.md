## Profile

Fullstack TypeScript Engineer and Web3 Engineer with **4+ years of commercial experience** building SaaS products, DeFi applications, high-load web platforms, and distributed systems.

I take products from technical discovery to production: domain modeling, system architecture, frontend and backend development, smart-contract integration, automated testing, observability, and deployment. I have worked with distributed international teams and can own complex technical areas independently while keeping implementation aligned with business requirements.

My core engineering focus:

- **Web applications:** scalable React, Next.js, Vue.js, and Nuxt.js frontends with SSR, predictable state management, and Feature-Sliced Design.
- **Distributed backends:** NestJS services, gRPC communication, Kafka-based workflows, background processing, caching, and data-intensive integrations.
- **Web3 systems:** Solidity, EVM-compatible networks, Lumia Chain, Ethereum, bridges, swaps, NFTs, and wallet-based authentication.
- **Platform engineering:** Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, CI/CD, secrets management, production diagnostics, and cloud storage.

A private full-stack rehabilitation management platform built around the workflows of patients, doctors, and administrators.

Its modular architecture spans the patient-facing frontend, administrative frontend, and NestJS backend, complemented by dedicated business and technical documentation.
